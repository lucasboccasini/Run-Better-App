A FEW GOOD STARTING POINTS

Tips you can use today

SAME RHYTHM

Choose a wind-down cue

Try a familiar order: dim lights, set tomorrow’s essentials aside, then pick a quiet activity.

GENTLE MOVEMENT

Unclench, don’t push

Try easy shoulder rolls, slow breathing, or a comfortable stretch. Stop if anything hurts.

WHEN SLEEP IS HARD

Keep the pressure low

If you’re awake, try a calm activity in a comfortable spot and return to bed when sleepy.

If sleep problems continue, affect your day, or you have concerns about your health, talk with a healthcare professional.
