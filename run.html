A FEW GOOD STARTING POINTS

Tips you can use today

EASY DAY

Keep it conversational

Slow down or add walking breaks until you can speak comfortably. Finish with energy left.

LONG RUN

Build time gradually

Choose a route and duration that match your current routine. Carry water for longer outings.

BEFORE & AFTER

Warm up and cool down

Begin with a brisk walk and gentle jog. Finish with a few minutes of walking.

New to running? Coach Lynn’s beginner plan above offers a structured start. If you have an injury or health concern, get personalized advice before increasing training.
