A FEW GOOD STARTING POINTS

Tips you can use today

POWERLIFTING

Practice the big lifts

Center training on squat, bench press, and deadlift strength. Plan sets, focus on technique, and avoid unsupervised max attempts.

BODYBUILDING

Train muscle groups

Combine compound and isolation movements, controlled repetitions, and enough rest for consistent sets.

CROSSFIT

Mix strength and conditioning

Sessions often combine skills, strength, and timed conditioning. Scale complex movements when tired.

RECREATIONAL

Build a balanced routine

Mix push, pull, squat, hinge, and carry patterns. Choose movements you enjoy and can repeat.

Use Build to select Powerlifting, Bodybuilding, CrossFit, or Recreational and generate a routine. Ask a qualified coach to show you unfamiliar equipment or technique.
